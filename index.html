Process

How the work gets done

  1. 01

    Audit the real markup

    Read every EJS partial before writing a line of CSS. Class names in the preview match the shipped templates exactly — what you see is what renders.

  2. 02

    Tokenize before styling

    Colors, radii, shadows and easings become custom properties first. Dark and light are two value sets over one structure — never two stylesheets.

  3. 03

    Verify with a real browser

    Headless Chromium screenshots at phone, laptop and full-HD widths, checked pixel-by-pixel. Reduced-motion and touch states are first-class, not afterthoughts.
